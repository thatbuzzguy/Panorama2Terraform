# Dual Licensing

This project has two licenses. You choose one, and the choice depends on how
you use the software. The license texts are the authority:
[`../LICENSE-AGPL`](../LICENSE-AGPL) and
[`../LICENSE-COMMERCIAL`](../LICENSE-COMMERCIAL). This page is a summary, not
legal advice. Legal review is out of scope for this project and is recorded in
[`../backlog.md`](../backlog.md).

## Option 1: AGPL v3

You may use, modify, and distribute the software under the GNU Affero General
Public License v3, at no cost.

Under AGPL v3 you must, when you distribute the software or a modification of it:

- release your source code, including your modifications, under AGPL v3;
- give the source to people who use the software over a network. This is the
  network copyleft rule (AGPL section 13), and it is the difference from GPL;
- keep the copyright notices, include the license text, and document the changes
  you made.

This applies to a hosted service or a tool you run for others. It does not stop
you from charging for the work.

## Option 2: Commercial license

The commercial license grants use under proprietary terms, for a fee. Under it
you may use the software in proprietary applications, modify it for internal use,
deploy it without releasing your source, and run it as a service, without the
AGPL obligations listed above.

`LICENSE-COMMERCIAL` states the grant, the license types, the fees, the warranty
terms, and how to request a license. This page does not restate them, because the
agreement is the authority and its terms can change.

## Which option fits your use

| Your situation | Option |
|---|---|
| Open source project, or you are willing to release your modifications | AGPL v3 |
| Learning or evaluating the tool | AGPL v3 |
| Internal use where you can publish the source if asked | AGPL v3 |
| Internal use where the source must stay private | Commercial |
| A product, a hosted service, or a tool you run for clients, where the source stays private | Commercial |
| Modifications you must keep proprietary | Commercial |

You can start under AGPL v3 and take a commercial license later. A license you
already followed under AGPL v3 does not become invalid.

## Compliance checklist for AGPL v3 use

- [ ] Ship `LICENSE-AGPL` with any distribution
- [ ] Keep the copyright notices
- [ ] Record the changes you made
- [ ] Provide the source to anyone who receives the software, or uses it over a
      network
- [ ] License your modifications under AGPL v3

## Compliance checklist for commercial use

- [ ] Hold a current commercial license for the entity that uses the software
- [ ] Follow the terms in `LICENSE-COMMERCIAL`
- [ ] Keep the license agreement with your records
